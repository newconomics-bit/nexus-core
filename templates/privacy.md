# PRIVACY POLICY

**RakitDigital** ("Provider", "kami", "kita") berkomitmen melindungi privasi **{{CLIENT_NAME}}** ("Client", "Anda").

## 1. DATA YANG KAMI KUMPULKAN
- **Account Info:** nama, email, phone, company name
- **Project Data:** requirements, specs, source code, documentation
- **Usage Data:** login timestamps, feature usage, error logs
- **Payment Data:** billing address, payment method (processed by payment processor)
- **Communication:** emails, chat logs, meeting notes

## 2. CARA KAMI MENGGUNAKAN DATA
- Provide & improve Services
- Process payments & billing
- Kirim notifications (billing, updates, security)
- Respond ke support requests
- Comply dengan legal obligations
- Improve platform (anonymized analytics)

## 3. DATA SHARING
Kami TIDAK menjual data Anda. Sharing hanya ke:
- **Sub-processors:** hosting (AWS/GCP), payment (Midtrans/Tripay), email (SendGrid)
- **Legal:** jika required by law/court order
- **Business Transfer:** merger/acquisition (dengan notice)

Semua sub-processors sign DPA (Data Processing Agreement).

## 4. DATA RETENTION
- **Active Account:** retain selama account aktif
- **Post-Termination:** 90 hari untuk data recovery, lalu deleted
- **Legal Hold:** retain longer jika required by law
- **Backups:** deleted dalam 30 hari post-deletion

## 5. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS (Anda berhak):
- **Access:** request copy of personal data
- **Rectification:** correct inaccurate data
- **Erasure:** request deletion (subject to legal hold)
- **Portability:** receive data in structured format
- **Restriction:** limit processing
- **Objection:** object to processing (marketing, profiling)
- **Withdraw Consent:** anytime (where consent basis)

Submit request ke: privacy@rakitdigital.com — respond dalam 30 hari.

## 6. SECURITY
- Encryption at rest (AES-256) & in transit (TLS 1.2+)
- Access control: role-based, least privilege, MFA
- Regular penetration testing & vulnerability scans
- Incident response plan: notify binnen 72 jam jika breach

## 7. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS
Data may be processed di Indonesia & Singapura (AWS/GCP regions). Adequate safeguards: SCCs, adequacy decisions.

## 7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
Services not directed to under 18. Kami tidak knowingly collect data dari children. Jika aware, kami delete immediately.

## 8. CHANGES TO POLICY
Kami may update Policy. Material changes: notify via email 30 hari before effective. Continued use = acceptance.

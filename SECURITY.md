# Security Policy

| Version | Supported          |
|---------|--------------------|
| v1.0    | :white_check_mark: |

## Reporting a Vulnerability

RakitDigital uses an internal AI Red Team agent ("Si Merah") to continuously test the security of our platform. We take security seriously and appreciate the efforts of security researchers who help keep our users safe.

If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it to us at **security@rakitdigital.id**.

Please include as much detail as possible, such as:
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- Affected components or endpoints
- Potential impact
- Suggested remediation (if any)

We do not perform public disclosure without your consent.

### Response Timeline

We will respond to your report within **48 hours**. We do not currently offer financial bounties, but we will list your name in our Hall of Fame if the report is valid and not previously known.

### Scope

In-scope:
- `api.rakitdigital.id` and subdomains
- RakitDigital web application and APIs

Out-of-scope:
- Third-party services (OpenRouter, Vercel, Supabase, Cloudflare, Telegram)
- Social engineering or physical security testing
- Denial of Service (DoS) testing

Thank you for helping keep RakitDigital secure.
